Release notes

Features and improvements in Threadseer for Power BI, with links to setup guidance and known limitations.

Threadseer 1.0.0.0

Last reviewed: September 9, 2026

Threadseer brings process discovery and investigation into one Power BI visual. Map Case ID, Activity, and Timestamp, then choose Analyze to explore how work moves through your process. Your report and process data stay in Power BI and are not sent to Wittenauer Software.

Explore and investigate your process

  • Process Map and Variants: discover the paths cases follow and inspect common or unusual routes.
  • Compare and Conformance: compare two process paths and review departures from the expected paths you define.
  • Drivers and Improve: explore patterns associated with outcomes and identify opportunities for further investigation.
  • Bottlenecks and Rework: locate delays, repeated activities, and loops.
  • Cases and Data Quality: trace findings to individual case timelines and review data conditions that affect interpretation.

Community and Professional include all ten analytical views, the same fields, and the same calculations. Professional adds higher data capacity. See Plans & Pricing for limits and licensing.

Working with Power BI

  • Choose Analyze to create a snapshot. When mapped data or filters change, keep the previous results visible until you choose to analyze the new selection.
  • If Power BI recreates the visual, Threadseer can automatically rebuild the last explicitly requested analysis when the data and mapped fields are unchanged, within the current license and data-capacity limits.
  • Use keyboard navigation and visible focus indicators across workspace controls, including Enter and Space selection on process-map transitions.
  • Inspect Power BI Value-field formatting in relevant map labels and tooltips.
  • When a read-only dashboard tile receives only part of a larger dataset, Threadseer directs you to the source report instead of loading indefinitely or analyzing incomplete data.

Known limitations and help

Review Known Issues and Limits for data capacity, dashboard behavior, case-detail display limits, and Power BI environment boundaries. The accessibility statement covers keyboard use and host-dependent behavior.
